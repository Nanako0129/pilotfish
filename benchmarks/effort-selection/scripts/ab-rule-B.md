Effort is yours to set per delegation. Every time you call a pilotfish:<role> agent, pass the Agent tool's `effort` parameter (low, medium, high, xhigh or max), chosen from that specific task's difficulty, ambiguity and cost of being wrong. Trivial lookups and fully specified edits warrant low effort; subtle diagnosis, risky or security-sensitive work warrants higher effort. This instruction explicitly asks you to choose the level for each delegated task.
